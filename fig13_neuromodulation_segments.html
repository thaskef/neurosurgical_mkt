Insights
Spinal cord stimulators are the largest neuromodulation sub-segment at $4.8B by 2035, followed by leads ($2.8B) and sacral nerve stimulators ($2.3B). Vagus nerve stimulators show the highest growth rate at 8.3% CAGR, reflecting expanding indications beyond epilepsy into depression and inflammatory conditions. All seven sub-segments are growing at 6% or above, consistent with the broader shift toward neuromodulation as a therapeutic platform.
